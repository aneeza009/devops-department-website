Admissions Overview

Find the program that fits your goals and learn about the eligibility, deadlines, and application steps.

How to apply

Submit a complete application with transcripts, statement of purpose, and any required test scores. Strong candidates demonstrate academic preparation and passion for computing.

  • Complete the online application form.
  • Upload academic transcripts and certificates.
  • Submit a personal statement and recommenders details.
  • Review eligibility and program-specific requirements.

Key dates

  • Fall intake: Applications open July 1, deadline August 15.
  • Spring intake: Applications open November 1, deadline December 10.
  • Offers announced: Within three weeks after review.

Entry requirements

  • Strong performance in mathematics and science courses.
  • Completed secondary education or equivalent for undergraduate entry.
  • Graduate applicants should have a bachelor's degree in a related field.
  • Motivation for computing, teamwork, and practical problem solving.
